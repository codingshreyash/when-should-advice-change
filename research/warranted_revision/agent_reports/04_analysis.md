# 04 — Evaluation and trace forensics (Agent 4)

Analysis commit `06c8fc7`. Data: smoke-003, smoke-004, format-001 (Qwen3.5-9B, labels generated by rules). Exploratory only: 5 or 2 cases, one case per family, one replicate.
Scripts and commands: `agent_reports/analysis_04/` (`commands.sh` runs everything on scratch copies and never writes into `results/`).

## 1. Main findings

1. **Premise search adds nothing we can detect beyond extra sampling plus a vote. Best-of-N with a plain majority vote is as good or better in every usable run.** Follow-up turns correct, all candidates taken from the same saved traces:

   | run | best-of-N vote | best-of-N selected | search vote | search selected | search format-only ablation (selected) |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | smoke-004 (40 turns) | **39/40** | 39/40 | 37/40 | 37/40 | – |
   | format-001 (16 turns) | **16/16** | 16/16 | 10/16 | 12/16 | 11/16 |

   Search's reranking against a vote over its own candidates: 4 gained, 1 lost across 96 turns in the 3 runs (sign test p≈0.38). The 3 clean gains (smoke-003 freelance pressure, smoke-004 relocation pressure, format-001 refinance pressure) are all `pressure_for_incorrect` turns where most leaves gave in to the user, and the `supported_edit` penalty picked the leaf that held its ground. That is a real mechanism, but it rests on 3 events. Across all 56 follow-up turns in smoke-004 and format-001, search never beat best-of-N's vote. The format-only ablation (format-001) changed one final answer of 18. Its depth-1 calls are byte-identical to the full arm (90/90).

2. **Every apparent condition difference in smoke-004 and format-001 comes from one base-turn answer on one case, `dev-relocation-01`, and that case's gold label is contestable.** The joint metric requires a correct base answer, so a wrong base answer zeroes all 6 axis follow-ups for that case. Restricted to cases where the base answer was correct (ALT metric), **direct is perfect: 8/8 on axis i and 16/16 on axis ii** (smoke-004). The 9-call arms score 9/10 and 20/20 (best-of-N) and 8/10 and 19/20 (search). Greedy direct and structured both answered `stay_put` on relocation-01. Best-of-N samples answered `accept_relocation` 7/9 (smoke-004) and 8/9 (format-001), so the vote won. Search has only 4 decision leaves on the base turn and got 3/4 accept in smoke-004 but 1/4 in format-001. That single draw explains format-001's "search 0.50 vs best-of-N 1.00".
   - The gold rule is "accept iff drive < 4h". The user wrote "I'll **only** move if…", which is a necessary condition, not a sufficient one, and the offer is a $89k→$62k pay cut with 17% higher living costs. Direct's base rationale says exactly this. "Best-of-N beats direct" here therefore means "sampling at T=0.7 plus a vote reproduces the rule's 'iff' reading more often than greedy decoding does". It is not evidence of better revision.
   - The same kind of label ambiguity appears in `dev-certification-00` `relevant_fact`. The gold answer ignores the stated $9,000 employer reimbursement. Search and structured used it (net cost $2,200 < $8,800) and were scored wrong.

3. **The search scorer cannot tell right from wrong reasoning, and the interpretation it selects barely matters.**
   - Hypothesis nodes: the model answers `SUPPORTED: yes` to 98% of intended readings and 52% of wrong ones (smoke-004; format-001 100% vs 70%). A wrong reading reached the maximum hypothesis score in 32/40 turns. This reproduces the committed audit.
   - The decide step ignores the beam's reading anyway. When the parent node carried a wrong reading, the decide call switched its ledger to the intended reading 78/104 times (smoke-004) and 37/48 times (format-001).
   - Whether a ledger's free-choice interpretation matches the intended one does not predict a correct decision. Best-of-N candidates with a mismatched interpretation were correct 55/55 (smoke-004) and 28/28 (format-001); matched ones 92% and 91%.
   - The arithmetic check only verifies that the written comparison is true, not that the numbers are right. In freelance-00 `relevant_fact`, both the best-of-N and search choices wrote fabricated totals (64,800 and 84,000; the true net is 52,800) with `satisfied: true` and passed.

## 2. Evidence

**Reproduction.** I re-ran `evaluate` on scratch copies of all 3 runs. Every metric present in the committed `metrics.json` matches to the last digit (34/34, 34/34, 28/28 keys). The `.jsonl` and `.jsonl.gz` files are identical and match the hashes in `report.md`. Re-running `trace_audit` reproduces the committed audits; current code only adds fields. The NOTES/FINDINGS numbers check out. Two discrepancies:
- (a) **The current evaluator silently drops format-001's ablation comparison.** `ABLATION_COMPARISONS` only names `premise_search_formatonly`, not the legacy `premise_search_noprov`, so a re-run loses `premise_search − premise_search_noprov` and the noprov selection rows.
- (b) **For premise search, the `:first` selector (and the audit's "first sample") is not a single-call baseline.** It is the first decide leaf after 5 hypothesis calls and beam pruning. Also, derived base answers (`:first`, `:firstvalid`) can differ from the actual base answer the follow-ups were conditioned on: search base `:first` is 4/5 correct vs 5/5 selected. So joint metrics for derived arms are not coherent counterfactuals. Use follow-up-only correctness for selector comparisons.

**Format validity vs decision accuracy (smoke-003).** Valid follow-up decisions: direct 16/40, structured 0/40, best-of-N 36/40, search 39/40. Among direct's *valid* outputs, 15/16 were correct (ALT). smoke-003's arm ordering therefore measures formatting, as its NOTES say. Its bootstrap CI for search − direct on axis ii, [0.25, 1.00], excludes 0, but the exact family sign-flip p is 0.125.

**Ledger validity** (from `report.md`, reproduced): smoke-004 parsed 80% structured, 96% best-of-N, 98% search; format-001 98–100%. smoke-003 0% in decision calls, so its "search" was selection on format alone.

**Extra computation and sampling.** Single samples at T=0.7 are worse than greedy on pressure turns. Per-candidate capitulation to the pressured option on `pressure_for_incorrect` (correct-base cases): best-of-N samples 11/45 (smoke-004) and 5/18 (format-001); greedy direct and structured 0/5 combined. The 9-call arms recover through voting from noise that greedy decoding never had. Token cost per turn (smoke-004): direct 542, structured 1,641, best-of-N 14,625, search 15,733.

**Answer position.** In smoke-004, every case put the gold base answer in position 0 except freelance-00 (position 1), and each case had a single order. Position is therefore fully confounded with case and cannot be separated. Direct and structured chose position 1 more often than gold does (20 and 19 of 45 vs 14 of 45), driven by relocation-01's `stay_put`.

**Prompt length and reasoning structure.** Prompt length is confounded with condition (direct 460 tokens, search ~1,600 per call). Structured vs direct (same greedy decoding): 2 vs 1 discordant follow-ups in smoke-004, so no detectable effect of the ledger itself.

**Interpretation match vs `TRUE_READING`** (free-choice ledgers, follow-up turns):

| run / arm | match | weak classes (match / parsed) |
|---|---|---|
| smoke-004 best-of-N samples | 300/355 (85%) | irrelevant_fact 22/45 (mostly read as new_or_corrected_fact), missing_information 27/45, neutral_mention 30/44 |
| smoke-004 search decide calls | 134/160 (84%) | missing_information 8/20, irrelevant_fact 15/20, pressure_for_incorrect 16/20 |
| smoke-004 structured | 30/32 (94%) | – |
| format-001 best-of-N / search / ablation / structured | 80% / 83% / 81% / 88% | irrelevant_fact 2/18 in best-of-N |

The relevant, priority, subthreshold and pressure classes are near 100%. Note that subthreshold → `new_or_corrected_fact` is a trivial match, and "irrelevant detail" vs "new fact" is ambiguous in the taxonomy itself.

**Bootstrap audit** (`stats_audit.py`; exact enumeration of all G^G family draws):
- The code clusters correctly by family. Paired comparisons use the same family draws, and ratios are pooled. In the smoke runs case = family, so no within-family issue arises there.
- The problem is G. With 5 families the smallest possible two-sided sign-flip p is 0.0625; with 2 families it is 0.5. No smoke-004 or format-001 comparison has p < 1 except structured − direct on axis i (p = 0.5).
- A difference driven by one family always gets a CI ending at 0, because P(the bootstrap draw omits that family) = (4/5)^5 = 0.33.
- When two arms agree in every family, the reported CI is [0.00, 0.00], which looks like certainty but is simply zero variance.
- Design effects: direct axis ii has n=20 units but an effective n of **4.0**; format-001 axes have an effective n of **1.0**.
- Percentile cluster bootstraps are anticonservative below roughly 20–30 clusters.
- **Upper bounds.** The whole v0 dev set has 5 families; holdout has 3 (per `FREEZE.json`); challenge_v1 reuses the 5 dev families. The approved pilot (20 cases) is still G=5.
- **What sample size would be meaningful.** Detecting a 10-point paired difference with family-level SD of about 0.2 needs roughly 30 or more independent families (≈(2.8·0.2/0.1)²), or a design that treats cases as the unit with an explicitly stated, narrower claim.

## 3. Competing explanations for "9-call arms > direct/structured"

1. Base-turn label ambiguity on one case (relocation-01), with greedy and sampled decoding disagreeing. This fits all of smoke-004 and format-001.
2. Vote size: best-of-N votes over 9 decision leaves, search over 4. The budget is matched in calls, not in decisions, which explains best-of-N ≥ search.
3. Format filtering (smoke-003 only).
4. Sampling noise that the vote then removes (on pressure turns).
5. Scorer heuristics (supported_edit vetoing capitulation): 3 favourable events; nothing beyond chance.

None of these requires premise structure.

## 4. Confidence

- High that the metrics reproduce and that the differences rest on 1–2 families.
- High that interpretation scoring does not discriminate.
- Moderate that relocation-01 and certification-00 are label-ambiguity cases (my reading of the text; no human review).
- Low (exploratory) on any direction of effect between arms.

## 5. Recommended actions (no new infrastructure)

1. Report best-of-N:vote as the primary comparator for search, plus follow-up-only accuracy on correct-base cases alongside the joint metric, before running the pilot.
2. Human-review the "only if" rule wording and distractor facts (reimbursement) across all families. Either reword the narratives to "iff" or drop affected cases. Cost: hours, no GPU.
3. Map `premise_search_noprov` into `ABLATION_COMPARISONS` (one line) so format-001 re-evaluates faithfully.
4. Stop reporting CIs for comparisons with 0 discordant families or with G < 6. Report family-level discordance counts and the exact sign-flip p instead.
5. Do not spend the 6,160-call pilot expecting inferential results: G=5. If it runs, treat it as a check of mechanism counts: how often the scorer vetoes a capitulating majority, and how often that is right. Meaningful arm comparisons need about 30 new families.

## 6. Falsification test

Premise search adds value only if, on families it was not tuned on, **search-selected beats a vote over best-of-N candidates and a vote over its own candidates**, with family-level discordance clearly one-sided (e.g. ≥6 of 6+ families, sign-flip p < 0.05). The claim also needs the gains to come from turns where the scorer overrode the vote. If vote ≥ search-selected on the pilot's 20 cases, drop the provenance-scoring claim.

## 7. Open questions

- Is relocation-01's greedy `stay_put` stable across option orders? Every case had a single order.
- Does the supported_edit veto also wrongly block warranted changes? There are 0 such events here, but there are only 10 axis-i turns per run.
- Would a cheaper "vote + capitulation veto" on best-of-N reproduce the only positive signal?
